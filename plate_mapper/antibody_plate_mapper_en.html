Antibody Plate Mapper

4 × 96-well plates · batch pairing → export antibody_map.xlsx

How to use (click to expand/collapse)
  1. Pick a "Numbering order" in the top right (controls how Well_name is auto-numbered) — usually set this once and leave it.
  2. Switch to the plate you want to fill in (Plate 1-4); edit that plate's Well_name prefix if needed.
  3. Click or drag-select a batch of wells on the grid; you can also click a row letter (A-H) or column number (1-12) to select a whole row/column, and Ctrl/Cmd-click to add non-contiguous wells.
  4. Fill in Tn5 modality barcode / nanobody_Tn5 / Histone_marker in the side panel and click "Apply" to write them to every selected well at once. You can also copy a whole column from Excel and paste it into the "Paste a column" box — it fills wells in order starting from the well you pick.
  5. Once all four plates are done, click "Generate & download antibody_map.xlsx" in the top right — only wells with all four fields filled in (barcode / well name / nanobody / histone) are exported as a row.

Everything you fill in is auto-saved to this browser on this device, so a page refresh won't lose it; it won't show up on a different device or browser, so treat the exported xlsx file as the source of truth.
